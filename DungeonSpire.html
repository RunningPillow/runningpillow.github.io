Dungeon Spire : Roguelike Co-op

Explore dangerous dungeons, discover powerful equipment, and build your own hero in a co-op roguelike role-playing game for Android.

Developed by Running Pillow, Dungeon Spire brings players together for dungeon runs, tactical turn-based battles, and character progression.

Get it on Google Play

About the game

Create a character build from randomized gear and discover combinations that help you overcome challenging encounters. Team up with other players, trade equipment, and compete in a shared player community.

  • Cooperative dungeon runs and tactical turn-based combat.
  • Character builds with varied equipment and powerful synergies.
  • Asynchronous PvP against other players’ AI-controlled builds.
  • Guilds, leaderboards, and an auction house for trading.

The game is available on Google Play and includes in-app purchases.
